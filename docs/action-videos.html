Individual action videos

Twenty visibly successful two-leg grasps, one unsuccessful diffusion-policy example, and one example each for IBC and LSTM-GMM.

Success here refers to visible two-leg capture in the recorded grasp phase. These clips do not show complete lift-and-rehang outcomes. Overlays are new offline checkpoint outputs with approximate image projection; the moving footage is the original recording. DP refines action sequences, IBC searches candidates, and LSTM-GMM predicts a one-step mixture distribution.
